Financial AI Agents

Agents, ETL, and RAG so accounting teams can query data in natural language.

Role
AI Engineer
Timeline
Sep 2024 — Feb 2025
Tools
CrewAI, Python, RAG, WhatsApp

At Dhart I built LLM systems so people could reach accounting data without waiting on a specialist. One of those systems sat on WhatsApp: users asked questions in natural language and got immediate, controlled access to the numbers they needed.

I created CrewAI agents and an ETL pipeline that automated data retrieval through text-to-code generation. Accounting staff spent about 40% less time pulling the same information by hand.

The RAG corpus was bloated. Through exploratory analysis I refined what actually belonged in it, cutting data load by over 90% and improving retrieval at the same time. Less noise in, better answers out.
