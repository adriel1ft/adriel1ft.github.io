Automatic Pitch Correction

Teaching a model to hear when a note is wrong — and to fix it without wrecking the take.

Role
Data Scientist Researcher
Timeline
Nov 2025 — Present
Tools
Python, ETL, audio ML

At Moises I work on the data and models behind automatic pitch correction. The job is not only to train a model — it is to get large music datasets into a shape the models can actually learn from.

I designed and implemented scalable ETL pipelines for those datasets: ingestion, preprocessing, and feature extraction. That automation is what feeds the company’s production models.

I also benchmarked automatic pitch-correction models, looking at audio quality and tuning accuracy so we could choose and improve what we ship. By refining dataset selection and introducing a targeted training strategy, pitch-correction accuracy moved from 73% to 88%.
