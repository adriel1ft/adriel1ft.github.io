Clustering Carbon Capture Hubs

Geospatial clustering to find high-potential carbon capture and storage sites.

Role
Data Scientist
Timeline
Sep 2023 — Sep 2025
Tools
Python, MeanShift, HDBSCAN, ETL

At TRIL Lab I worked on where carbon capture and storage infrastructure should actually go. The input was geospatial and emissions data; the output was a clearer map of high-potential hubs.

I built ETL pipelines, ran exploratory analysis, and trained MeanShift and HDBSCAN models on treated data to classify those hubs. Visualizations sat on top so the results could support siting decisions, not just live in a notebook.

The models improved selection of high-yield sites by over 25% — a practical gain in a domain where picking the wrong location is expensive.
