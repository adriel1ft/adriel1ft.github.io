Shrinking OCR Models

Same output, far less hardware — and a way to prove the quality didn’t quietly drop.

Role
Data Scientist Intern
Timeline
Jul 2025 — Oct 2025
Tools
PyTorch, GPTQ, Hugging Face, Vertex AI

OCR models are expensive to run. At Dharma-AI I optimized ours with GPTQ quantization, which cut GPU usage by 40% and made inference 20% faster.

The obvious risk with quantization is that quality degrades in ways that are hard to spot. So I validated the output with an LLM-as-a-Judge framework, which gave us a repeatable way to say the smaller model was still good enough to ship.

A lot of the work was upstream of the model. I ran EDA and sanity checks on high-volume Portuguese text data, finding patterns and stripping noise, because OCR improvements are only as good as what you measure them against.

I also engineered specialized coding datasets and enriched them with Google Vertex AI, which sped up the preparation phase for training small language models.
